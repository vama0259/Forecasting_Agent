# Industry Analysis & Multi-Domain Platform Roadmap

## 1. Vision: The Cross-Industry AI Analyst
Platform architecture generalizing beyond financial markets into a universal **Autonomous Industry Analysis & Planning Platform**.

## 2. Cross-Domain Data Topologies
| Industry | Data Shape | Specialized Algorithms | Key Driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Finance (MVP 1)** | Continuous OHLCV, participant flows | GARCH, XGBoost, Participant Intent Debate | Conviction & directional probability |
| **Supply Chain / Retail** | Intermittent, zero-inflated, hierarchical | Croston, Tweedie, Hierarchical MinT Reconciliation | Inventory holding cost & stockout prevention |
| **Energy & Utilities** | High-frequency telemetry, weather series | NeuralForecast, spatio-temporal transformers | Peak load & capacity dispatch |
| **Enterprise FP&A** | Multi-entity relational ledgers, budgets | Scenario-elasticity models, driver-based trees | Variance attribution & cash flow runway |

## 3. Enterprise Integration Pillars
1. **Private Data Ingestion:** Autonomous SQL, Snowflake, Databricks, and Parquet profile connectors replacing web scraping.
2. **Prescriptive Actionability:** Connecting probabilistic forecast distributions directly to inventory/hedging optimization engines.
3. **Collaborative Planning (S&OP):** Ingesting qualitative human commercial overrides as constraint parameters in agent debate rounds.
4. **Driver Attribution:** Automated SHAP and factor-waterfall decomposition for executive explainability.
